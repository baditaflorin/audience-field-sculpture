Welcome

This artwork uses your phone's camera to detect a printed tag near a sculpture, then responds with visuals and ambient sound. Nothing leaves your device.

  • The camera feed stays on this phone. No uploads, no analytics.
  • The microphone is not used.
  • You can close the camera at any time from the menu.
