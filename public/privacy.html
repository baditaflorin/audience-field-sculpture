Privacy

Audience Field Sculpture is built to respond to people without watching them. This page describes exactly what the artwork does and does not do with your phone.

What the artwork uses

  • Camera — frames are read from your camera, analysed on this device for a printed sculpture tag, and immediately discarded. They are never uploaded, stored, or sent anywhere.
  • Local storage on this device — used only to remember your settings (volume, expected tag id, debug toggles, etc.) so the artwork behaves the same the next time you open it. Cleared by tapping Reset all settings.

What the artwork does not use

  • No microphone. The artwork plays audio, it does not listen.
  • No face recognition, no biometric profiling, no identity tracking of any kind.
  • No analytics, no telemetry, no third-party scripts.
  • No accounts, no logins, no server-side database.
  • No GPS or location services.
  • No Bluetooth or peer-to-peer connection to other phones.

How "collective" behaviour works

The artwork is designed so that several people can experience it together near the same sculpture without any of their phones communicating with each other. Each phone is independent. The shared experience comes from each phone reacting to the same physical scene at the same time, not from any signal exchanged between them.

Source code

The full source is at github.com/baditaflorin/audience-field-sculpture. The version of the artwork you are running is shown in the app's menu, with a link to the exact commit it was built from.
